The COSMOS-Web Lens Survey (COWLS)

Welcome to the COSMOS-Web Lens Survey (COWLS), over 400 strong-lens candidates found in JWST imaging of the COSMOS field, each with four-band NIRCam images, lens models and downloadable data.
