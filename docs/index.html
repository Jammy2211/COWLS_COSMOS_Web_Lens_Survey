The COSMOS-Web Lens Survey (COWLS)

Strong-lens candidates found by visually inspecting ~42,000 JWST NIRCam cutouts from the 0.54 deg² COSMOS-Web survey. Each candidate has four-band JWST imaging (F115W, F150W, F277W, F444W), a PyAutoLens lens model for most, and archival HST, MIRI and ground-based data. M25 holds the 17 spectacular lenses of COWLS II; S12–S00 are candidates ranked by their visual-inspection score, from very high to very low quality. Click a thumbnail for that lens's images, model results and downloads.
